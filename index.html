Market Opportunity & Revenue Model

Over 280,000 Australian hair, beauty, wellness, fitness, and boutique studio operators rely on appointment scheduling. Incumbent platforms increasingly charge per-staff seat fees plus marketplace commissions.

  • High-Margin B2B SaaS Subscription: Recurring $149/mo Business and $500/mo Ultimate AUD subscription tiers collected via Stripe Billing.
  • No Funds Custody Risk: End-consumer service fees are settled directly in person between the customer and the merchant; MateSlot does not hold or intermediate consumer funds.
  • Lean Multi-Tenant Infrastructure: Lightweight Node.js + SQLite WAL multi-tenant architecture running behind Cloudflare edge caching and SSL.
